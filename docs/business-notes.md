## Ranking Rules

1. All albums shown on the Home page must also be present on either the Top 10 page or the Top 20 page, based on actual critic and public rankings.
2. The Top 10 page must contain exactly 10 albums per era.
3. The Top 20 page must contain exactly 20 albums per era.
4. Eras are: **Early Pioneers** (pre-1980), **1980s**, **1990s**, **2000s**, **Present Day** (2010s–2020s).
5. The Soundtracks page is exempt from the ranking rules above; it is an editorial collection rather than a ranked list.
6. Soundtracks are always sorted chronologically by release year, and standalone albums that also served as movie or game soundtracks are eligible for inclusion.
